### The Next Chapter

As I transition into my 7th semester of the B.Tech CSE program, it is time to shift gears toward our official minor project. I am incredibly excited to announce that for the upcoming semester, my team and I will be diving deep into the intersection of healthcare and machine learning.

### The Project Scope: XAI in Medical Imaging

Our project is titled **"Medical Image Analysis with Explainable AI (XAI)"**.

While deep learning models have achieved remarkable accuracy in analyzing medical scans, their inherent "black box" nature makes them difficult for medical professionals to trust in high-stakes environments. Our goal is to build computer vision pipelines that not only detect pathological features but also _explain_ their reasoning—visually highlighting exactly which regions of an image contributed to a specific diagnostic output.
